How Clemistry works

Everything on the shelf carries real data: a structural formula, a standard enthalpy of formation, a heat capacity, and where relevant an acid dissociation constant or a solubility product.

  • Structures are built from the connectivity, not drawn by hand. Each atom is given the shape VSEPR predicts from its steric number, rings are seeded as polygons, and the whole molecule is then relaxed. Water comes out at 104.7°, ammonia at 107°, benzene flat with equal bonds.
  • Energy is never invented. The heat of any reaction is the difference between the enthalpies of formation of its products and its reactants, and that heat goes into the contents according to their heat capacities.
  • Solutions are modelled as ions. pH comes from solving the electroneutrality condition, so weak acids behave like weak acids; precipitates appear when the ion product passes the solubility product; metals displace each other according to the activity series.
  • Conditions matter. Some reactions need igniting, some need a catalyst, some need a current or ultraviolet light, and some simply need heat.

Try: zinc in hydrochloric acid · silver nitrate with sodium chloride · hydrogen peroxide with manganese dioxide · aluminium with iron(III) oxide, then ignite.
